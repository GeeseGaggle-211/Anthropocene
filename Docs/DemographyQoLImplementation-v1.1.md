## Welfare and survival are separate

Composite QoL remains the experienced-welfare signal. Its food, water,
clothing, housing, expectation and social-cohesion terms continue to drive the
Elder objective, economic valuation, immigration and fission. QoL is never an
argument to the physical birth/death kernel.

`DemographicModel.PhysicalMortalityV1` is the new-world default. Its continuous
annual law is:

```text
dN/dt = (b - d) N
b = 0.045 F
d = d_exposure + d_crowding + d_disease + d_legacy_starvation + d_other
d_exposure = 0.03255509888111371 (1 - 0.45 C - 0.45 H)
Phi(N;k) = expm1(kN) / expm1(1000k)
d_crowding = 0.03130836758391647 Phi(N; 0.00001)
d_disease = 0.010436122527972156 Phi(N; 0.000027161883008306556)
```

`F`, `C` and `H` are realized food, clothing and shelter service in `[0,1]`.
The v1.1 fertility factor is exactly one. There is no fitted background death,
population floor, mortality ceiling, free provision, demographic correction
bonus or survival-shaped reward. Crowding and disease are zero at zero
population, split 75:25 at 1,000, and are equal at 100,000. A fully served
1,000-person settlement is an exact stationary root with nonzero gross births
and deaths.

The existing starvation rule remains a named compatibility cause:

```text
d_legacy_starvation(F) = -ln(1 - 0.18 (1 - F)) / 20
```

It begins after 40 physical years of continuous food deficit. Full nutrition
resets the saved deficit duration immediately; there is no later birth
prohibition. The legacy discrete subtraction and this continuous hook cannot
both execute.

## Paid service and population propagation

Food and textile service close against the person-years generated by the same
population path. For `Y = integral N dt`:

```text
F = min(1, paid_food / (food_per_person_year Y))
C = min(1, paid_textiles / (textiles_per_person_year Y))
H(t) = min(1, shelter_people_capacity / N(t))
```

Budgets contain only inventory, production, imports and provisions already
committed by the real turn. The demographic kernel cannot plan, manufacture,
trade, harvest or replay ecology. Stored typed food that closure does not use is
returned by provenance. Unused local expedition forage is discarded as a
flow; unused carried provisions return to the expedition.

The scalar path integrates `x = ln N` on deterministic 0.05 log-population
bands, inserting equilibrium and shelter thresholds as knots. It uses stable
`expm1`/`log1p` forms and four-point Gauss-Legendre accounting for person-years,
births and each death cause. Starvation-grace expiry splits the physical
interval exactly. Root, band, closure and finite-domain failures throw
`DemographicNumericalException`; they never clip the hazard or population.

### Coupled settlement density

A settlement can contain a camp cohort and temporary expedition cohorts. It is
not valid to remove an expedition from crowding merely by dispatching it.
`AdvanceCoupledBudget` therefore evolves all cohorts from one source settlement
with one shared, time-varying total density:

- the camp and connected expeditions share the camp food, textile and housing
  service pool;
- each detached expedition has its own local/carried food pool and no camp
  clothing or shelter;
- every pool still contributes to the same crowding/disease density;
- each biological cohort retains its own deficit age and starvation boundary.

Mathematically exact proportional service/history splits reduce to the scalar
propagator bit-for-bit. Heterogeneous schedules use a bounded adaptive
log-vector RK4 step-doubling solver with Richardson correction. Error control
covers every log population and every accounting integral; service pools close
together through a bounded damped fixed point. Step, service and finite-domain
budgets fail explicitly. Tests compare the vector path with an independent
small-step RK4 reference and cover arbitrary interval composition, 100,000
population decline, multiple pools and distinct deficit ages.

The heterogeneous RK4 path is a reviewed numerical-method extension around the
contract-selected analytical scalar propagator, not that original analytical
method itself. Exact scalar reduction is preserved; broader vector accuracy,
finite-budget reference and mature performance evidence remain promotion gates.

The full-service shortcut requires the physical budget to meet the birth-only
upper envelope exactly; no absolute epsilon can turn a microscopic zero budget
into free food or textiles. A homogeneous scalar solve's work counters are
attributed to exactly one non-empty pool, so production diagnostics retain the
shared work without multiplying it by temporary partitions.

At the integer boundary, a connected settlement keeps one shared gross-event
rounding ledger so splitting or rejoining cannot manufacture a whole birth or
death. Within that conserved total, completed events are allocated from each
cohort's own continuous birth/death burden, with the sub-half-person extinction
rule applied once to the settlement total rather than once per temporary
partition. Both fractional ledgers follow a surviving connected entity if
burden-based allocation rounds the camp cohort away. This prevents one cohort's starvation from being
spread over unrelated people while retaining dispatch/control parity.

## Turn-boundary conservation

Gross births and gross deaths have separate saved fractional ledgers. Only
their integer net changes residents and workforce, so equilibrium turnover does
not erase specialist roles. Production, needs and storage settle against the
locked starting cohort; demographic events commit afterward. For a standalone
cohort—or the shared ledger of a connected settlement—a completed death is
deferred when it would erase the represented population while its continuous
endpoint is at least 0.5. Temporary partitions do not each receive that
protection. A declining represented endpoint below 0.5 rounds to zero and
reports the one aggregate continuous residual actually discarded and its
physical causes. A connected cohort that rounds away while the shared ledger
survives reports no independent extinction. This is an explicit representation
convention, not a population floor.

Dispatch, return, immigration and fission conserve people and transfer the
physical gross-event/deficit state applicable to the physical model. Connected
reintegration weights deficit histories by continuous cohort mass, independent
of which integer entity received the rounding remainder. The legacy path
ignores these physical-only ledgers exactly as before.

## Save and migration boundary

`DemographicModel.LegacyQoLNetGrowth` has value zero. A serialized
configuration without the selector therefore loads the historical behavior and
is never silently reinterpreted. New configurations and `OpeningConfig.json`
select `PhysicalMortalityV1` and disable the legacy QoL-mortality lever.

`DemographicMigration.ToPhysicalV1(world, reason, sourceCommit)` is the only
conversion API. It converts legacy deficit turns to physical years and appends
a fingerprinted `DemographicMigrationRecord` containing reason, source commit,
clock, entity counts and population. Duplicate, reasonless or source-less
migrations fail.

## QoL migration and fission

`Immigration.ForwardUtility`, `Fission.QoLModel.ForwardAt`, destination ranking,
the immigration deadband/size rule, hysteresis, fission gain thresholds and the
Elder reward retain their existing calibration. A higher-QoL destination with
worse mortality can still attract people. Physical mode projects mortality and
net growth from food, clothing, housing, population and inherited deficit age
directly—never composite QoL—and records negative-net and
higher-QoL/worse-mortality cases.

These projections are diagnostic only. An instantaneous negative net rate is
not equivalent to physical nonviability: a well-served settlement above its
positive equilibrium declines toward that root, and a temporarily distressed
settlement may recover. Consequently the physical model adds no post-QoL social
veto, survival weight, reranking, group-size change or threshold. Focused checks
cover both an above-equilibrium daughter with negative net growth and a move
from a worse declining source to a safer but still-declining receiver.

Fission daughters inherit the parent's expectation and accumulated deficit
history. People, typed food, portable inventory, gross-event progress and herds
retain the existing proportional conservation rules; fixed housing remains at
the parent site. Immigration retains its existing movable-goods set and also
transfers proportional physical ledgers and population-weighted deficit
history. No arbitrary survival weight or new reward term was introduced.

## Diagnostics and RL boundary

`DemographicReport` records model identity, continuous and integer gross
births/deaths, every cause, person-years, `F/C/H`, effective rates, scalar and
coupled work counters, equilibrium/extinction state, cause correction and
conservation error. Observation schema 19 exposes the owning settlement's QoL,
nutrition, protective coverage, deficit age, fertility/mortality and cause
rates; it does not reveal foreign private state.

`SettlementWelfareReport` records local population, QoL and
population-weighted QoL. `LineageWelfareReport` separately reports lineage-wide
population-weighted and mean QoL plus cross-lineage immigration and fission
transfers. These are diagnostics only. They make jurisdiction changes visible
without changing the authorized objective.

The native GO gate and serious-training gate are separate. Numerical parity is
not sufficient: native conservation, recoverable distress, real-economy
recovery, viable settlements and acceptable migration/fission behavior must
pass first. A learned Elder additionally needs a matched runtime/checkpoint,
observation/normalizer migration, action-interface validation and a manifest
pinning the native simulator commit.

`PlanningResidentPopulation` is currently a homogeneous/local scalar
projection used by checks, not the live Elder. It does not model detached shared
density or connected cohorts with distinct deficit ages and must not be treated
as general coupled forecast/resolution parity or wired into RL planning without
an explicit coupled forecast contract.
