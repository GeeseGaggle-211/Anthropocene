# Issue #26: bounded food and cultivation forecasts

Implementation base: `c519cd4` on the shared `codex/issues-26-28-33-35` branch. This change supplies physical one-horizon forecasts for the existing economic policy and a future Elder caller. It does not choose Elder preferences or change the harvest-first resolver order.

## Authority and time

Saved `Food`, `PotentialFood`, cultivation capital/operation, tile ecology timestamp, camp inventory and workforce are authoritative at turn boundaries. `FoodHorizonForecast.Tile` reads an explicit world and, when provided, an `EcologyView` captured at the accepted opening boundary. The optional opening view allows field endpoint reconciliation to score alternatives against the locked prior state after other tiles have committed. Forecast results, known claims and work counters are transient.

Land receipts debit opening human-accessible `Food` and opening herd-accessible nonfood biomass. The four seasonal profiles force a single bounded analytical recovery for the next boundary; neither field expansion nor managed renewal adds retroactive current harvest. Aquatic food retains its separate coupled horizon stock-flow path. `FoodHorizonForecast.Tile` calls `EcologicalPressure.Integrate` with its Wood sidecar omitted, so a food query does not initialize the global material index. The production resolver keeps its default Wood projection.

`Settlement` uses the resolver's agricultural claim constructor at an explicit accepted camp location. For a mobile camp or accepted village departure it instead uses the resolver's passive claim constructor, including the existing `GatherFraction` limit and bridge-level access cap on overlapping former fields. The caller supplies candidate labor, biological and plant-feedstock targets, an optional field allocation and only known local competing claims. The result labels incomplete contention as an optimistic envelope. It reports terrestrial and aquatic receipts separately, and `MealFood` subtracts terrestrial plant manufacturing input once. An accepted village departure requires zero field labor and forecasts former owned fields at zero operation; a blocked move uses the original site. Illegal farming labor without Agriculture is rejected.

`Investment` compares two calls to the same tile kernel. It reports gross natural and managed forcing separately from retained next-boundary Food, and annualizes the retained delta over the chosen single horizon. Land gross forcing sums the actual ordered recovery profiles; complete seasonal cycles are multiplied analytically, so the ledger does not approximate them with a mean-capacity product. The allocator divides that annualized gain by its existing build cost plus `PlanningHorizonYears` weighted operation cost. It assumes no later re-harvest. This replaces the former gross-flow score that could reward managed input lost to capacity saturation, without changing utility weights or adding a new safe-use threshold.

## Work and scale

One tile query evaluates at most the fixed four seasonal profiles plus bounded edge/reclassification work, independent of years or `BaseDays`. A settlement query visits local eligible/field tiles and supplied local claims: `O(local tiles + known local claims)` rather than map area, global camps, population, or simulated historical duration. An investment comparison uses two tile queries. Candidate field allocation retains its existing fixed intensity bands and local footprint bound. Gross-forcing accumulation is opt-in on the authoritative integrator and allocates no callback in ordinary resolution. Detailed counters are also opt-in and never enter authoritative state; timing, allocations and counters cannot change results. Mature-empire and RL callers should batch their local observations and avoid repeated identical candidate comparisons; any order-of-magnitude increase in field or candidate count warrants another scale audit.
