# Paleolithic completion and ecology foundation

This replaces Milestone 3 ecology and ownership rules. The reconciled [Drive plan](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bghyyEUw55bhM0NQRyzHP6RlfU5JM6LV/edit), particularly section 14, and the user decisions in [EcologyImplementationPlan.md](EcologyImplementationPlan.md) are authoritative. Starvation has two player turns of grace; subsistence shares are balance targets, not quotas. The historical DesignSpec.txt is unchanged.

Environment, vegetation and relief are separate. Seasonal resources are generated after soil, environment and oasis placement. Natural vegetation is a generated target; current vegetation follows configurable condition hysteresis.

`SeasonData.RegionalMoisture` is the climate water balance without a local water subsidy. `SeasonData.Moisture` is actual local soil moisture; land productivity, land renewable capacities/rates, habitat and observed soil-moisture bands continue to use it. Aquatic food uses the separate profile described below. Both moisture fields are authoritative normalized seasonal floats. The extra field costs 16 payload bytes per tile (8 MiB for Standard), with no extra per-tile objects. Neither value is a per-turn resource stock.

Generation runs the existing three warm-up cycles in season order, starting both moisture accumulators at 0.3. For each season, forcing is `7 * Rain + snowmelt - 0.002 * max(0, Temperature)`, with snowmelt equal to twice the previous season's Snow when the current temperature is above zero. Regional moisture becomes `clamp(0.35 * previousRegional + forcing, 0, 1)`; local soil becomes `clamp(0.35 * previousSoil + forcing + subsidy, 0, 1)`. The subsidy is 0.65 for lakes, otherwise `0.65 * clamp(maxIncidentRiverEdgeFlow / RiverThreshold, 0, 1)`. Generated rivers already meet that threshold, so their former soil-water contribution is preserved. Weak constructed flows get less water. Use actual boundary-edge flow here, not the independent tile drainage proxy. Oasis placement subsequently raises only local soil to at least `OasisVegetationMoisture * OasisInfluence` (default 0.45 at the spring).

Land classification uses mean temperature and mean **regional** moisture: below -10°C is Ice, below 3°C Cold, moisture below 0.12 Desert, below 0.32 Grassland, then temperature above 21°C Tropical, otherwise Temperate. These are configurable thresholds. Testing the grassland moisture threshold before tropical temperature also keeps hot dry savanna in Grassland. Water environments retain their existing identity.

Natural vegetation uses `warmth = clamp((meanTemperature - IceTemperature) / VegetationWarmthRange, 0, 1)` and `score(m) = warmth * clamp((m - VegetationDryFloor) / VegetationMoistureRange, 0, 1)`. Scores below 0.30 are Low, below 0.60 Medium, otherwise High (configurable). Climatic tier uses mean RegionalMoisture. Hydrologic uplift is the seasonal mean of `max(0, Moisture - RegionalMoisture) * clamp(MaxFreshwater / 70, 0, 1)`; saltwater or no freshwater source gives zero. Local tier uses regional mean plus uplift. Natural potential is the greater of climatic tier and local tier capped at Medium; current natural Desert generation is additionally capped at Medium. Thus High always requires regional support, and a river flag alone cannot guarantee Medium. The 70-unit water reference and 0.65 soil subsidy are provisional opening-generation tuning, not physical units.

Within VegetationResourceStage, seasonal water/productivity profiles now finish before potential is classified; potential still precedes gatherable-share/biomass initialization. No inter-stage order changes. Desert + Medium + a visible river is labelled “Riparian vegetation”; Forest/Rainforest/Taiga remain derived labels. Later local water interventions can modify soil and capacities independently; actual climate shifts can change regional moisture and reclassify environment. No irrigation, dams, salinity or later-era mechanics are implemented here.

The map represents standing natural cover as no trees for Low (grass/brush only), one tree for Medium and three for High. Cultivation retention derives the standing-cover tier from the raw ecological tier, with no natural vegetation sprites at full displacement (`q=1`). This presentation convention does not overwrite ecological vegetation. Tree counts remain fixed for each displayed tier while detail is visible, then disappear at distant zoom.

Aquatic food is generated by `AquaticEcology`, independently of soil moisture, rainfall and snow. Coastal Shallows use a cool-water nutrient/mixing proxy: fertility is `0.70 + 0.60 * clamp((22 - meanTemperature) / 18, 0, 1)`. Warm coasts retain the 0.70 baseline; cool coasts gain up to 0.60. This is informed by nutrient-rich coastal upwelling ([NOAA](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/upwelling/)), but does not simulate currents or claim cold alone universally determines fishery productivity. The generated temperature is an atmospheric seasonal proxy, not measured seawater temperature.

Local variation multiplies fertility by `0.85 + 0.30 * keyedRandom(seed, 142, region)`, with fixed 16-by-16 tile regions. The seasonal light factor is `clamp(1 + 0.75 * normalizedLatitude * sin(season * pi/2), 0.25, 1.5)`, sharing ClimateStage's hemispheric phase. Coastal open-water factor is `clamp((seasonTemperature + 8) / 8, 0.04, 1)`. These coarse cold/light availability proxies limit extreme-cold productivity; they do not create ice terrain, change traversal or add a physical sea-ice simulation. The small floor avoids a hard seasonal extinction rule.

Freshwater lakes instead use fertility `0.65 + 0.20 * clamp(meanTemperature / 20, 0, 1) + 0.10 * catchment`, where catchment is `clamp(log(1 + max(0, Flow)) / log(1 + 10 * max(1, RiverThreshold)), 0, 1)`. Lake open-water factor is `clamp((seasonTemperature + 5) / 10, 0.04, 1)`; the same variation and light factors apply. Lakes receive modest warmth/catchment support and retain finite freshwater. They do not inherit the marine cool-water bonus.

Generator 11 extends these climatic profiles with the [aquatic food web](AquaticFoodWeb.md). Ordinary Shallows have High plankton/algae, 25% human-harvestable biomass and a 0.65 renewal fraction per BaseDays before climate scaling; Ocean has Low plankton, 10% accessibility, five times ordinary shallow biomass and a slower 0.08 renewal fraction. Lakes retain their climate response and renewal input, with Medium plankton and 50% accessibility. Land-adjacent Shallows cannot upwell. Outer-shelf Shallows with no land neighbor receive graded upwelling from adjacent Ocean: up to fivefold capacity and twice shallow renewal. Generated food and biomass remain separate, conserved pools. Aquatic condition recovery uses stored biological turnover instead of land vegetation. Ocean remains excluded from opening harvest and viability; freshwater and shared gathering labor limits are unchanged. These are generation proxies, not physical currents or nutrient transport.

Start viability reads radius-one land stock and all four seasonal profiles. For each land tile, it depletes opening human food `G`, applies one turn of the same four-profile recovery as resolution, and credits at most the original opening `G`. This screens for repeatable food rather than a one-time initial stock. Lake and adjacent Shallows retain their renewable-flow estimate; Ocean and elevation-10 peaks are excluded. The starting gatherer share is `min(1, Fraction(GatherFraction, GenerationIntervals) * StartingPopulation / PopulationScale)`. The combined land/aquatic estimate is capped by one full turn of starting gathering labor, then divided by the starting population's full-turn consumption. `StartViability = 85` means this conservative estimate covers at least 85% of starting food need. Opening reserves and nearby prey cover the intended margin; eligibility does not promise survival under every order or competing claim. A transient per-tile cache avoids repeating the land projection for nearby candidate sites.

Freshwater eligibility additionally requires radius-one nonsaline, sourced capacity of at least `StartFreshwaterMinimum` in **every** season, and mean seasonal recharge `sum(MaxFreshwater * WaterRecoveryPerDay * BaseDays) / 4` of at least that threshold. A full but nonrenewing pool, a dry season without another source, or productive saltwater alone cannot pass. The starting tile must be traversable land; separation and reachable-herd placement checks remain intact. This generation-only calculation uses stack scratch space plus a transient land-food cache and never materializes or mutates ecology. Both land hydrologic improvements and revised fisheries affect eligibility through their actual resource profiles.

Food is the human-accessible subset `G` of total edible biomass `B`. On land, humans and herds draw from opening stock before any food recovery: humans can take only `G`, while herds take `B-G` first and then residual `G`. All realized receipts debit one nonnegative tile ledger. Land recovery then composes the four seasonal climate profiles analytically over the turn and prepares next-turn stock; an operated field adds managed recovery to `G`. Endpoint capacity clamps enforce `0 <= G <= B` and prevent either stock from exceeding its current field and condition ceiling. Freshwater and aquatic food retain their separate horizon flow. The land recovery calibration and measured outcomes are in [LandFoodRecoveryCalibration.md](LandFoodRecoveryCalibration.md).

SeasonData recovery fields are per-day rates. Generation configuration fractions (`LandFoodRegeneration`, `FoodRegeneration` for lakes, `WaterRecovery`, and oasis equivalents) are converted when seasonal profiles are created using `-ln(1-f)/BaseDays`; loaded worlds keep their stored rates. Generator 13 uses land fraction 0.03, separate from lake fraction 0.4, and scales new land biomass and gatherable stock with `LandBiomassMultiplier=30`. Habitat gatherable fractions do not change; cultivation reaches `G=B` at `q=1`. Biomass, gatherable food and freshwater remain finite: realized harvest and drinking debit their stock; the saved seasonal recovery rates replenish bounded stock over elapsed time. `LandCondition` remains stored and continues to scale land capacity, but current gathering, grazing, cultivation, timber and quiet projection neither damage nor recover it. Existing saves keep their recorded value. Historical land condition damage/recovery coefficients remain in configuration for compatibility and future disturbance design; they do not write land condition in this model. Generator-11 aquatic condition retains its independent turnover-based recovery and damage rules. Pressure entries and tile balances are transient diagnostics; saved history retains transfers, condition, logistics and demographic explanations without serializing the scratch ledger.

Cultivation adds managed renewable production at `CultivationRegenFlux * q * BiomassCapacity * BiomassRecoveryPerDay * operatingFraction`, with `BiomassCapacity` still scaled by the stored `LandCondition`. The shared sustainable harvest rate is `BiomassCapacity * BiomassRecoveryPerDay * SustainableProductionFraction + ManagedFlow`; pressure diagnostics compare realized human-plus-herd extraction per day with this daily safe rate. Only a tiny, duration-independent rate epsilon guards division by zero. The managed term is counted once and is not reduced by `SustainableProductionFraction`. Planning forecasts and pressure use the same ecology helper. Current land extraction may exhaust food stock but cannot alter `LandCondition`; a later explicit disturbance system may use that retained field.

The cultivation land-cover overlay begins above `CultivationVegetationDisplacementStart = 0.50`. Natural vegetation retention is one through `q=.50`, then `(1-q)/.50` through `q=1`. It scales the wild-food contribution and ecological Wood carrying capacity, while the raw ecological `CurrentVegetation` tier remains available for climate, succession and forest/woodland build and maintenance labor. The food capacity composition is `WildFoodNatural + (BiomassCapacity - WildFoodNatural) * q²`, where `WildFoodNatural` is the existing ecological wild-food capacity times retention. Thus a healthy `q=1` field can support cultivated biomass to its ecological ceiling while providing no standing natural food or timber service. Expansion clamps existing Wood to the lower cap; abandonment relaxes the cap as cultivation capital decays, and Wood regrows only through its duration-aware recovery. No extra land-cover or soil state is saved.

Quiet tiles retain latent stocks and LastEcologyUpdateDays. ProjectEcology is pure; four-profile quiet cycles compose affine transforms algebraically, with bounded partial-cycle work and endpoint hysteresis. MaterializeEcology commits that same projection before extraction. Observations and human details project visible tiles only. Production resolution checks affected tiles; full validation remains available to audits/load. Saving retains dormant timestamps and never regenerates geography.

One player decision still resolves camp/hunter/herd movement once (1/2/1 tiles), provisions, hunting, one integrated demographic update and progression. Ecology seasons are internal math. Two player turns of starvation grace are intentional. Gatherer claims retain the prior cohort, accessibility and labor-cap formula. Land food availability is bounded by opening `G`; recovered land food enters the next turn. Human claims share a deterministic, ordered useful-food budget per camp: feed current camp residents and in-supply hunters, refill their complete `GrowthReserveTurns` operating reserve subject to local storage capacity, then fund the food implied by the maximum configured growth rate. Existing inventory and already-realized hunting are credited before connected fishing and passive gathering claims are proportionally scaled. Remote hunters consume and carry their own provisions and are not duplicated in camp reserve demand. Remote fishing is limited by remaining expedition carry space; local foraging remains limited to current consumption. Thus realized human tile food is consumed or retained rather than later counted as discarded inventory.

Intentional regression changes: next-season capacity is checked through projection rather than requiring every stored tile to advance. Proficiency-impact fixtures explicitly exceed the safe-use band. Skilled aquatic harvesting may yield more food because lower damage preserves productive capacity.

Society owns technology, practice, mandates and era state. Settlements retain local people, workforce and inventory. Mastery can complete the era early; majority eligibility begins at 200 years and forced completion at 240. Opening transition effects are an explicitly optional compatibility adapter with owned seed/improvement records. Workforce deaths use proportional largest remainder, with stable gatherer-first/ordinal-role tie breaks.

Current Neolithic save signature is ANTHROPOCENE-11; the separate resource block was introduced with the ANTHROPOCENE-9 envelope and older ANTHROPOCENE-8 envelopes remain readable. The current world/config version is 7 and this experimental land profile uses core generator 13; earlier generator saves retain their exact stored capacities/rates when loaded, while future versions and saves predating separate regional moisture remain rejected. Observation schema is 14: owned domestic-herd camp links, generation diagnostics, quantized local candidate-site coverage, and owned agricultural field-payoff estimates are visible to the owner, while foreign and wild private state remains filtered; observed Moisture still means local soil water. Filtered observation/action DTOs and legal action masks share the human validator. Society policy inclinations persist independently of player labels; mandates, practice and observed opportunities drive specialization. A planning snapshot indexes occupants and recent own extraction once, and AI planning runs on the resolution worker.

Validation evidence is recorded in [Validation.md](Validation.md); [ConfigurationUnits.md](ConfigurationUnits.md) audits the tuning fields and [Performance.md](Performance.md) records the scale limits.

PhaseExecution provides a scalar reference and optional bounded parallel workers. AI/herd intent phases read a planning index; ecological workers read materialized tile inputs and return local results. Sequential simulation barriers commit in tile/entity order on the resolution worker. Movement collisions, ID allocation and history remain canonical. Worker counts, partition order and timings are not saved state. Current desktop execution uses at most four workers and avoids parallel dispatch below 64 items.


The map uses nine broad ground/water families with separate climate-specific vegetation motifs, land-use hooks, settlement overlays and distinct unit/species icons. Rivers and merged elevation contours remain procedural; shorelines use observed neighbours only. Both contrast sets retain glyph fallbacks and the same semantic keys. Zoom removes vegetation and land-use detail before occupants/contours; actor artwork stays inside the exact selectable slots, with transparent negative space and open selection corners. [ArtProvenance.md](ArtProvenance.md) records the 29-sprite cartographic set and its relationship to the recovered visual reference.

Paleolithic claims retain radius-one gathering, finite stores, passive adjacent Shallows, one strategic relocation and food-limited population growth. Food allocation is strictly consumption, full operating-reserve refill, then growth. Ordinary consumption is based only on the pre-growth population. The final bucket uses the current exponential demographic equation to calculate the food needed for the realized rate; births are applied afterward and never consume or acquire reserves retroactively. Hunting remains an integer-animal abstraction: a kill is made only when the complete carcass yield fits camp acceptance or expedition carrying capacity; prey left for lack of capacity is not recorded as a failed hunt. The default starting population is 24 with one complete generation's reserve; there is no human population cap. First-generation dispatch creates an eight-person group in the documented demo; it becomes active in the following planning phase. Owned people and livestock are exact; wild/foreign herd counts remain estimated.

Passive aquatic harvest credits its practice weight equally to Gathering and Fishing (default `PassiveAquaticFishingPracticeShare = 0.5`). Successful retained hunting and unsuccessful hunting practice both use the base `HuntingPracticeMultiplier` (3.25); there is no additional successful-hunt experience bonus. Species exposure, active fishing and other domains' normalized rewards retain their existing rules. Overflow hunting food earns no practice. This changes experience only, not harvest quantities, food stores or resource regeneration. Saves retain their stored passive-share setting.

The opening scope ends at the first breakthrough. The separate Neolithic implementation provides cultivation intensity and specialist production; it does not enable daughter-settlement creation, diplomacy, trade, warfare or RL training. Weather, habitats, opening-herd guarantees and climate-based storage horizons remain approximations/gameplay scaffolds.
