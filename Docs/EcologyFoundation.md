# Paleolithic completion and ecology foundation

This replaces Milestone 3 ecology and ownership rules. The reconciled [Drive plan](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bghyyEUw55bhM0NQRyzHP6RlfU5JM6LV/edit), particularly section 14, and the user decisions in [EcologyImplementationPlan.md](EcologyImplementationPlan.md) are authoritative. Starvation has two player turns of grace; subsistence shares are balance targets, not quotas. The historical DesignSpec.txt is unchanged.

Environment, vegetation and relief are separate. Seasonal resources are generated after soil, environment and oasis placement. Natural vegetation is a generated target; current vegetation follows configurable condition hysteresis.

`SeasonData.RegionalMoisture` is the climate water balance without a local water subsidy. `SeasonData.Moisture` is actual local soil moisture; productivity, renewable capacities/rates, habitat and observed soil-moisture bands continue to use it. Both are authoritative normalized seasonal floats. The extra field costs 16 payload bytes per tile (8 MiB for Standard), with no extra per-tile objects. Neither value is a per-turn resource stock.

Generation runs the existing three warm-up cycles in season order, starting both moisture accumulators at 0.3. For each season, forcing is `7 * Rain + snowmelt - 0.002 * max(0, Temperature)`, with snowmelt equal to twice the previous season's Snow when the current temperature is above zero. Regional moisture becomes `clamp(0.35 * previousRegional + forcing, 0, 1)`; local soil becomes `clamp(0.35 * previousSoil + forcing + subsidy, 0, 1)`. The subsidy is 0.65 for lakes, otherwise `0.65 * clamp(maxIncidentRiverEdgeFlow / RiverThreshold, 0, 1)`. Generated rivers already meet that threshold, so their former soil-water contribution is preserved. Weak constructed flows get less water. Use actual boundary-edge flow here, not the independent tile drainage proxy. Oasis placement subsequently raises only local soil to at least `OasisVegetationMoisture * OasisInfluence` (default 0.45 at the spring).

Land classification uses mean temperature and mean **regional** moisture: below -10°C is Ice, below 3°C Cold, moisture below 0.12 Desert, below 0.32 Grassland, then temperature above 21°C Tropical, otherwise Temperate. These are configurable thresholds. Testing the grassland moisture threshold before tropical temperature also keeps hot dry savanna in Grassland. Water environments retain their existing identity.

Natural vegetation uses `warmth = clamp((meanTemperature - IceTemperature) / VegetationWarmthRange, 0, 1)` and `score(m) = warmth * clamp((m - VegetationDryFloor) / VegetationMoistureRange, 0, 1)`. Scores below 0.30 are Low, below 0.60 Medium, otherwise High (configurable). Climatic tier uses mean RegionalMoisture. Hydrologic uplift is the seasonal mean of `max(0, Moisture - RegionalMoisture) * clamp(MaxFreshwater / 70, 0, 1)`; saltwater or no freshwater source gives zero. Local tier uses regional mean plus uplift. Natural potential is the greater of climatic tier and local tier capped at Medium; current natural Desert generation is additionally capped at Medium. Thus High always requires regional support, and a river flag alone cannot guarantee Medium. The 70-unit water reference and 0.65 soil subsidy are provisional opening-generation tuning, not physical units.

Within VegetationResourceStage, seasonal water/productivity profiles now finish before potential is classified; potential still precedes gatherable-share/biomass initialization. No inter-stage order changes. Desert + Medium + a visible river is labelled “Riparian vegetation”; Forest/Rainforest/Taiga remain derived labels. Later local water interventions can modify soil and capacities independently; actual climate shifts can change regional moisture and reclassify environment. No irrigation, dams, salinity or later-era mechanics are implemented here.

The map represents current vegetation as no trees for Low (grass/brush only), one tree for Medium and three for High. This is a presentation convention; it does not change ecological tiers or resource calculations. Tree counts remain fixed while vegetation detail is visible, then disappear at distant zoom.

Food is the human-accessible subset G of total edible biomass B. The resolver integrates G and non-gatherable B-G in ordered seasonal segments. Generated profiles give both components the same renewal rate, so their sum follows dB/dt = g(K-B)-H. Humans have first claim on G; herds consume B-G before spilling into G. Freshwater is a separate finite stock. Depletion uses an analytic zero crossing and ongoing recharge; unmet requests are recorded. Capacity loss at seasonal boundaries is accounted for as overflow.

SeasonData recovery fields are per-day rates. Legacy configuration fractions (FoodRegeneration, WaterRecovery and oasis equivalents) are converted exactly once while generating profiles: -ln(1-f)/BaseDays. They remain migration inputs, not per-turn multipliers. ConditionRecoveryPerYear exactly preserves the old six-percent recovery over twenty years, before potential-based low/high modifiers. SafeUseRatio and SustainableProductionFraction are dimensionless; use damage is annual. Realized extraction is compared with a conservative fraction of renewable flow. Drinking has zero condition damage. Failed requests have zero damage. Pressure entries carry source, category, requested/realized/unmet flows, capacity exposure, recharge and integrated damage contribution; tile balances reconcile all three pools. These are transient diagnostics retained for the latest resolved turn. Saved history preserves transfer receipts, condition changes, logistics and demographic explanations; it does not serialize the transient ledger or numerical-balance scratch records. This keeps large-world saves bounded without losing continuation state.

Quiet tiles retain latent stocks and LastEcologyUpdateDays. ProjectEcology is pure; healthy quiet cycles compose capped affine transforms. Damaged tiles recover across ordered local season segments and update hysteresis on demand. MaterializeEcology commits that same projection before extraction. Observations and human details project visible tiles only. Production resolution checks affected tiles; full validation remains available to audits/load. Saving retains dormant timestamps and never regenerates geography.

One player decision still resolves camp/hunter/herd movement once (1/2/1 tiles), provisions, hunting, one integrated demographic update and progression. Ecology seasons are internal math. Two player turns of starvation grace are intentional. Gatherer claims retain the previous population, accessibility and labor-cap formula; availability includes cumulative renewable flow, not merely final standing stock.

Intentional regression changes: next-season capacity is checked through projection rather than requiring every stored tile to advance. Proficiency-impact fixtures explicitly exceed the safe-use band. Skilled aquatic harvesting may yield more food because lower damage preserves productive capacity.

Society owns technology, practice, mandates and era state. Settlements retain local people, workforce and inventory. Mastery can complete the era early; majority eligibility begins at 200 years and forced completion at 240. Opening transition effects are an explicitly optional compatibility adapter with owned seed/improvement records. Workforce deaths use proportional largest remainder, with stable gatherer-first/ordinal-role tie breaks.

Current save signature: ANTHROPOCENE-8; opening-v7.anthro; world/config/generator 7. Older saves lack separate regional moisture and are preserved and rejected explicitly. Observation schema remains 7 because its fields are unchanged; observed Moisture still means local soil water. Filtered observation/action DTOs and legal action masks share the human validator. Society policy inclinations persist independently of player labels; mandates, practice and observed opportunities drive specialization. A planning snapshot indexes occupants and recent own extraction once, and AI planning runs on the resolution worker.

Validation evidence is recorded in [Validation.md](Validation.md); [ConfigurationUnits.md](ConfigurationUnits.md) audits the tuning fields and [Performance.md](Performance.md) records the scale limits.

PhaseExecution provides a scalar reference and optional bounded parallel workers. AI/herd intent phases read a planning index; ecological workers read materialized tile inputs and return local results. Sequential simulation barriers commit in tile/entity order on the resolution worker. Movement collisions, ID allocation and history remain canonical. Worker counts, partition order and timings are not saved state. Current desktop execution uses at most four workers and avoids parallel dispatch below 64 items.


The map uses nine broad ground/water families with separate climate-specific vegetation motifs, land-use hooks, settlement overlays and distinct unit/species icons. Rivers and merged elevation contours remain procedural; shorelines use observed neighbours only. Both contrast sets retain glyph fallbacks and the same semantic keys. Zoom removes vegetation and land-use detail before occupants/contours; actor artwork stays inside the exact selectable slots, with transparent negative space and open selection corners. [ArtProvenance.md](ArtProvenance.md) records the 29-sprite cartographic set and its relationship to the recovered visual reference.

Paleolithic claims retain radius-one gathering, finite stores, passive adjacent Shallows, one strategic relocation and food-limited population growth. The default starting population is 24 with one complete generation's reserve; there is no human population cap. First-generation dispatch creates an eight-person group in the documented demo; it becomes active in the following planning phase. Owned people and livestock are exact; wild/foreign herd counts remain estimated.

The current scope ends at the first breakthrough. Society/settlement sharing, named workforce roles, transition seeds, generic pressure integration and filtered action/observation contracts are foundations for later work. They do not enable daughter-settlement creation, deliberate domestication, cultivation intensity, specialist production, diplomacy, trade, warfare or RL training. Weather, habitats, opening-herd guarantees and climate-based storage horizons remain approximations/gameplay scaffolds.
