# Neolithic craft validation

`Tools/NeolithicCraftValidation` is an opt-in, reproducible laboratory for the physical craft chains and natural post-unlock economy. It does not alter gameplay tuning or unlock policy. The controlled recipe witnesses and normal-policy cohorts are reported separately because a finite feasibility witness does not establish that the live policy will choose that chain.

## Model and scale

Authoritative state remains the saved turn-boundary world: camp and society inventories/technology, prior-cohort population, workforce orders, fields, herds, and ecology. The natural window begins at the resolution where the society first gains Cultivation or AnimalHusbandry. That boundary is excluded; each policy/control branch then resolves exactly 25 later turns from a byte-identical save. Demands come from the `PreparedTurn` prior-cohort snapshot, production and extraction work from actual turn receipts, and field work from optional counters at the realized claim/field commit sites. The existing joint farmer reservation is reconciled as `max(maintenance, realized harvest equivalent) + distinct expansion`; the diagnostic does not claim a separate upkeep/harvest schedule that the simulation does not represent. Gathering that supports field bridging is counted once as gathering; its bridge credit is annotated separately.

The natural cohort uses generated 128×64 worlds with eight players. Discovery, replay rows, and optional dictionaries scale with captured unlocks, active craft/material receipts, and the fixed 25-turn horizon, not with all historical turns. The diagnostic counters are disabled by default and are neither saved nor exposed through observations. The separate parent performance harness validated 80- and 320-tribe runs; 320 tribes is the measured large-scale point, and RL training throughput was not independently measured. This runner is a bounded behavior audit, not a new production simulation pass.

## Run

From the repository root in PowerShell, using the checked-in or Unity-bundled .NET 8 SDK:

```powershell
$dotnet = '.tools/dotnet/dotnet.exe'
& $dotnet run --project Tools/NeolithicCraftValidation/Anthropocene.NeolithicCraftValidation.csproj -- `
  --source-label candidate --source-revision <measured-revision> `
  --repo . --simulation-source Assets/_Anthropocene/Simulation `
  --out TestResults/NeolithicCraftValidation/Candidate --seed-limit 3 --max-discovery-turns 60
```

The baseline must compile the same runner against the archived simulation sources, while reading the same current opening/resource configuration. Diagnostic-only references are behind `ISSUE33_DIAGNOSTICS`, so baseline compilation uses `-p:EnableCraftDiagnostics=false`. Give the baseline a separate output path and rebuild so it cannot overwrite the candidate binary:

```powershell
$baseline = (Resolve-Path 'TestResults/Issues26-28-33-35/BaselineSource/Assets/_Anthropocene/Simulation').Path
& $dotnet build Tools/NeolithicCraftValidation/Anthropocene.NeolithicCraftValidation.csproj `
  --no-restore -t:Rebuild -p:SimulationSource=$baseline `
  -p:EnableCraftDiagnostics=false -p:OutputPath=bin/Issue33Baseline/ `
  -p:UseSharedCompilation=false
& $dotnet Tools/NeolithicCraftValidation/bin/Issue33Baseline/Anthropocene.NeolithicCraftValidation.dll `
  --source-label baseline --source-revision c519cd4f1d35520bb0788171f8fb3e9c851a6dcf `
  --repo . --simulation-source $baseline --input-unlock-saves TestResults/NeolithicCraftValidation/Candidate `
  --skip-witnesses --out TestResults/NeolithicCraftValidation/Baseline
```

`--source-revision` is mandatory so an archived source tree is not mislabeled with the host checkout's Git HEAD. Each manifest stores the declared measured revision, host checkout HEAD/status, source/config/runner hashes, exact command line, cohort counts, and observed route/habitat counts. `unlock-saves.jsonl` stores the exact candidate unlock checkpoint bytes and SHA-256. `--input-unlock-saves` lets the baseline replay those same bytes, so the post-unlock before/after comparison does not confuse different discovery states. Compare only rows with matching checkpoint hashes and unchanged config/resource-file hashes. The archived baseline already includes the older #21/#27 changes, so this comparison cannot identify their causal effect.

## Evidence files

- `recipe-witnesses.csv` contains finite source-backed chain attempts for all nine recipes: 17 productive controls (eligible generalist plus matching mastered specialist) and one real, blocked generalist attempt for SpecialistOnly Tools. Each productive control makes 0.25 units initially and in each of two subsequent service/replacement periods. It records extraction and production receipts, tool use, one-input-missing negatives, and the actual replacement receipts; two identical demand queries are only a repeatability check, not recurrence evidence. Animal-food-dependent controls resolve a managed herd through the real turn pipeline and preserve the `AnimalFood -> AnimalFabric -> LightHousing` chain without granting AnimalFabric. The CSV labels the disclosed food stocks and source receipts.
- `unlock.csv` records each live unlock rule, the projected local habitat at the actual unlock boundary, local Stone/Clay/Wood source opportunity, source-shock status, and mobility. The natural cohort has 21 observed unlocks and three censored camps in the 3-seed final run. `censored.csv` keeps dead or not-yet-unlocked natural societies visible. Four separately labeled generated-site controls hold one camp stationary through activity-driven discovery at pinned river, open, woodland, and dry sites; their habitat is reclassified at actual unlock, not inherited from a site label.
- `trajectory.csv` and `trajectory.jsonl` contain exactly 25 turns per boundary and branch: useful good demand, engine forecast/realized output and unmet demand; accepted role assignments and receipt-backed work; food use, manufacture, reserves and growth; tool/material provenance; field q/E, projected local food/biomass, shelter, and land condition; movement and satisfaction; specialization/mastery events and end-state constraint classifications based on captured pre-resolution priorities/technology. Farm-work fields are explicitly unavailable in the archived baseline because those diagnostics did not exist there; cumulative capacity clipping is also unavailable because the core exposes no exact cumulative counter.
- `window-summary.csv` reports first useful output/mastery, first specialist assignment, sustained productive specialist work (including actual material extraction), and the labor audit for each branch. `summary.md` reports observed natural coverage, generated-site controls, and censored cohorts.
- `replay.csv` records per-unlock checks of three later turns under scalar and four-worker resolution with reversed entity order, a mid-window save/load continuation, plus a controlled legal-stay diagnostics-off/on fingerprint comparison. Baseline builds report the diagnostic parity as not compiled.

The three natural branches retain the same unlock save. `policy` leaves the focal order unchanged. `legal-move-auto-economy-mobile` deliberately submits one deterministic legal non-stationary move (the lowest-destination option if policy did not already choose a move) with automatic economy enabled; this is forced mobile movement, not normal policy movement. `source-aware-schedule-stationary` submits exactly one focal Economy order generated by the existing source-aware planner and no movement order. The runner verifies the accepted order(s) before it counts a window. Four generated-site controls provide explicitly conditioned site coverage, not normal placement frequency. Three additional matched controls set each local source pool for Stone, Clay, or Wood to zero once at the same captured natural unlock; they are disclosed exogenous opening-stock shocks, preserve deep stock, and do not repeatedly suppress Wood regrowth. Natural missing strata remain gaps: the final sample has no natural dry-marginal unlock, exactly four of 21 natural unlocks have no locally known Clay source (zero `source_clay`), Stone and Wood quantities are positive at all 21, and no natural simultaneous Cultivation+AnimalHusbandry unlock. Habitat names use the radius-one footprint and projected current vegetation, with the same thresholds for natural and generated-site controls.

## Measured 3-seed run

The candidate run used source revision `5e34e7f`, three generated seeds, and a 60-turn natural discovery cap. It found 21 natural activity-driven unlocks (15 Cultivation, 6 AnimalHusbandry) and censored three camps. Natural unlock habitat counts were 13 woodland, five river, and three open; dry-marginal and simultaneous-route natural unlocks were absent. Four separate pinned-site stationary-discovery controls supplied one actual Cultivation unlock in each habitat class. All four initial source sites had positive Stone, Clay, and Wood; separate matched shocks reduced each material to zero at one natural unlock, recorded in `unlock.csv` and the per-source receipts in `unlock-saves.jsonl`.

The candidate and archived baseline each completed 84 windows and 2,100 trajectory rows. All 84 exact checkpoint pairs had byte-identical unlock-save hashes and identical opening/resource config-file hashes; `SaveCodec.ConfigurationHash` differed because the archived and candidate simulation schemas serialize different defaults, which the reducer records rather than treating as a same-state mismatch. The candidate produced 18 recipe-witness rows, passed 25 three-turn scalar/four-worker/reordered/save continuation checks, and all 2,100 candidate rows reported accepted focal actions and labor within the prepared prior-cohort budget. The baseline explicitly marks farmer/field-work diagnostics unavailable; its farmer labor comparisons are excluded. Candidate/baseline simulation-source SHA-256 values were `39006a739c2ca56fe3050e1f33bd83c955244684b7ed86d9ea401a27c42b99c3` / `9bd92c88254e6a0f4d6baade61802dc6660e186ff41d6f03e8de25d8d4a4fea8`; runner assembly SHA-256 values were `f5eed8dd11d38ea3e0f5778181715e3fbb62819be0ff9595646c9a9b80eb6273` / `4dcfe4d825f9e22dba127df61034f7ad43a1d5591c88844ebf6a7b35d479a71c`.

Natural mean QoL by route and branch was: Cultivation policy 0.489 candidate / 0.489 baseline, stationary source-aware schedule 0.461 / 0.461, forced legal movement with automatic economy 0.699 / 0.652; AnimalHusbandry policy 0.540 / 0.540, stationary source-aware schedule 0.326 / 0.326, and forced legal movement with automatic economy 0.719 / 0.719. These are equal-weighted means across unlock windows, not population-weighted outcomes. The forced-movement contrast changes location and access to opportunities, so higher QoL does not establish that the branch satisfied more underlying needs or predict ordinary policy behavior. The archived source already contains #21/#27, so these results do not measure their causal effects.

Natural-policy specialist employment was uneven. Across the 21 natural boundaries, Toolmaking had receipt-backed specialist production in all 21 windows (median 18 productive turns; maximum continuous streak 22); Weaving had productive work in 17/21 (median 17 turns; maximum streak 21); Construction in 4/21 (maximum streak 5); Pottery and Carpentry had no productive specialist turns. These are only the first 25 turns after each unlock and do not establish later mastery or long-run adoption. Material-extraction roles are counted separately from manufactured output in the checked-in specialist reduction.

Shelter remained a measured failure mode despite generally strong food and water satisfaction: in natural `policy` windows, mean housing satisfaction was 0 in 14/15 Cultivation windows (route mean QoL 0.489; mean food satisfaction 0.999) and 3/6 AnimalHusbandry windows (route mean QoL 0.540; mean food satisfaction 0.995). The Stone-zero shock was especially severe for the stationary comparisons: both `policy` and source-aware stationary produced no useful goods in all 25 turns and mean housing satisfaction/QoL were zero; the forced-movement branch produced output after moving. This one matched boundary shows that mobility can reach other opportunities, but it does not isolate a general Stone effect from the bundled movement/economy treatment. The Clay-zero and Wood-zero shocks retained output in the 25-turn sample; Wood continued to regenerate after its one-time shock.

These observations do not resolve issue #23/#24 policy attribution. The experiment starts after discovery, retains existing priorities/automation, and its contrasts bundle mobility or source shocks with an established planner; it does not independently vary the disputed policy rule. The 320-tribe performance run validates that scale for the measured simulation workload, but RL training throughput was not independently measured. Do not infer the latter from the former or from this 128×64 behavior cohort.

## Validation limits

The controlled witness uses a fixed 500-resident camp and small 0.25-unit production probes. Non-husbandry cases disclose 100,000 prior PlantFood; herd cases disclose 40,000 opening PlantFood (one current meal horizon), then receive 800 accepted AnimalFood from a real `TurnResolver` managed-herd receipt. Raw and manufactured inventories are zero before and after that provenance turn; source-backed extraction begins afterward. The two continuation periods apply real service/wear and finite-source replenishment, with no births or deaths. These probes establish recipe feasibility and recurring replacement paths only. They do not meet all 500 residents' needs, demonstrate a self-sustaining village, or predict natural policy adoption. Natural results report policy behavior, not a guarantee that every route unlocks in every seed. The model's farmer upkeep/harvest overlap is retained and labeled because it is an existing joint operating reservation. Any over-budget reconciliation remains visible in `labor_audit`; it is not clamped into apparent productive/idle time. Post-consumption input constraints that cannot be attributed from opening stock and source receipts are labeled unresolved. No claim is made for missing natural strata, absent specialist emergence, or any source/input chain that fails its assertions.

The `ScarcityControls` helper clones one captured unlock save and sets every locally known source pool for Stone, Clay, or Wood to zero once at that boundary. Its serializable result logs source IDs, tiles, shares, scope, and before/depleted/after amounts; deposit-body source IDs share one accessible pool across their sites, so the receipt exposes the local share of that pooled stock. This is an exogenous starting-stock shock, not labor or extraction. It leaves geological deep stock and source initial budgets untouched. Wood's ordinary saved renewal remains active after the one-time shock, so later Wood availability is regenerated exposure rather than another depletion.
