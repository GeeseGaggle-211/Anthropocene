## Explicit supersessions and unresolved edges

- **Seasonal authority:** four seasonal profiles remain forcing. Seasonal tick loops and 90-day player-turn claims are obsolete; see #29 for unresolved cross-era harvest cadence.
- **Need cohort:** prior-cohort resident needs are locked at preparation. Issue #2's old post-growth Fabric exception is superseded for both recipe demand and clothing saturation; `TurnNeeds.Production` locks the prior cohort for every recipe.
- **Clothing feedstock order:** food is allocated to consumption, then the clothing-need feedstock (`NeolithicProduction.ClothingNeedFeedstock`, shared by `EconomicPlanner`, `CampProductionDemand.Build`, `TurnPlanner` needs and `PlanGrowth`), then the operating-reserve refill, then growth with every other manufacturing input (user decision 30 September 2026; see the clothing collapse diagnosis in [ValueModelImplementationPlan.md](ValueModelImplementationPlan.md)). Validated by `ClothingFeedstockChecks.cs` (fixed suite).
- **Tool chronology:** the analytical resolver explicitly permits source extraction, then toolmaking, then equipment-using manufacture in the same horizon. The two fixed recipe layers are an acyclic input balance, not dated economic ticks. All targets and available labor remain locked to the prior cohort; #33 measures useful output and blocked paths without changing this chronology.
- **Land damage/recovery:** legacy land-condition coefficients can remain serialized/configurable for compatibility while current land flows leave `LandCondition` unchanged. This is deliberate current scope, not validated ecological equilibrium and not an approved future disturbance model.
- **Movement/economy:** ordinary accepted village movement abandons village status and releases the old footprint's operation. Earlier “villages cannot leave” prose is obsolete. Daughter-society fission is implemented (WP10a, below); Camp Elder and husbandry decisions remain #23/#24.
- **Contact knowledge (WP6):** a Society past its first breakthrough (`Era != Paleolithic` or `Technology != None`) keeps saved, optional `Society.Memory`: a sparse 8x8-block bitset of explored tiles and contacts (camp, owner, last-known tile, turn, `Sighting`/`Trade`/`Kin`, Moved) in camp-ID order. `ContactKnowledge.EndTurn` runs last in `TurnResolver.Resolve`, after movement and placement, from each Society's own post-turn vision: it marks visible tiles and each camp's hinterland (radius `VisionRadius` growing `HinterlandRingsPerTurn` per stationary turn up to `VisionRadius * sqrt(N / HinterlandReferencePopulation)` and `HinterlandMaxRadius`), forgets 8x8 blocks not refreshed for `ExploredMemoryTurns` (5), records or refreshes foreign camps in view (Sighting), marks a contact Moved (tile unknown) when its recorded tile is in view without it, and drops contacts unrefreshed for `ContactExpiryTurns` (5) turns. Paleolithic Societies keep live vision only. `RecordTrade` is called by trade settlement ([Trade.md](Trade.md)); `RecordKin` is an API hook; kin copies the parent's knowledge. **Remembered knowledge (explored static geography and known settlement snapshots) is a deliberate, labelled exception to the filtered-observation rule**: observation schema 17 adds `RememberedTiles` (terrain/environment, relief, elevation level, ridge links, water kind, rivers only) and `KnownSettlements`; remembered tiles never carry vegetation state, stocks, herds or occupants, and `TileDetails.Describe` labels them REMEMBERED. Unity map rendering of remembered tiles is not yet implemented. Design: [QoLFissionTradeDesign.md](QoLFissionTradeDesign.md) 3.7; details and measurements: [ContactKnowledge.md](ContactKnowledge.md). Checks: `ContactKnowledgeChecks.cs`.
- **Fission (WP10a, push recalibrated in WP10c):** each camp has a saved, optional `FissionPolicy` (Neutral, the enum's zero, / Discourage / Encourage: expected-gain threshold `FissionGainThreshold` / `FissionGainDiscourage` / `FissionGainEncourage`, daughter 15 / 30 / 40 percent of residents as exact integer `floor(N * pct / 100)`, affinity 0.5 / 0.9 / 1.0) and a saved, optional cached `FissionDestination` (tile, regime at caching, turn). `Fission.Resolve` runs in the Close phase after demography, progression and breakthrough, before `ContactKnowledge.EndTurn`. The push is crowding plus expected gain: `Fission.EstimateSplit` computes, from the camp's committed terms and the `ResolveNeeds` formulas, the stayers' QoL after the daughter leaves (F, W and the expectation unchanged, clothing coverage unchanged, social cohesion at `N - N_d`, housing per head recomputed with all fixed Housing kept and portable Light Housing split in proportion) and the relative gain `G = Q_after / Q_now - 1` (when `Q_now = 0` the fallback is the cohesion gain `S_after / S_now - 1`). A camp searches lazily (ring of traversable land within `FissionRadius` that the Society knows: Paleolithic current vision only, later vision plus explored tiles; at least `MinimumCampSpacing` from every camp) when `G >= FissionSearchFraction * G*(policy)` and no valid cache exists; sites are scored `D = clamp(SiteSupport(daughter cohort) / daughter need, 0, 1)` by the start-viability footprint evaluation per regime and ordered by `D` descending, then the uncapped ecological support ratio descending, then hex distance, then tile ID; the cache is invalidated when occupied, within spacing, `D < StartViability / 100` or the regime changes. A proposal needs `G >= G*(policy)`, a cached viable destination, a daughter of at least `FissionMinimumDaughter` that is smaller than the remaining parent, and the daughter check: the estimated daughter QoL (F = W = 1, unchanged clothing coverage, housing from its portable share, the post-move expectation) must be at least `Q_now - FissionDaughterMargin`. Proposals are evaluated on bounded workers from the frozen snapshot and committed in camp-ID order (a conflicting proposal is dropped, keeps its cache, and is never rerouted). The commit creates a new Society, AI player slot (`WorldState.PlayerCount`, never reused) and camp, and moves residents, typed food, all inventory, fractional demographic progress and domestic herds in the daughter's people fraction while the parent keeps site, fields and fixed Housing; knowledge, practice and opening status (era, technology, progression, transition state) are copied (kin contacts via `ContactKnowledge.RecordKin`), lineage is stored as `Society.ParentSociety` / `KinAffinity` (siblings take the lower affinity, derived), and the daughter runs continuous economic automation under Neutral policy. `WorldState.Finished` is decided by founding Societies only: a daughter (`ParentSociety > 0`) never delays the end of the opening. Observation schema 18 adds owner-only policy, cached destination, lineage and kin. `FissionPolicy` is a legal action (`OrderKind.FissionPolicy`). Older saves load with fission disabled and their fixed player count. Fission remains opt-in (`FissionEnabled = false`). Fission is QoL-driven, not crowding alone (**distress fission**): the stayers' estimate includes food and water relief (`F_hat = min(1, F N / N_stay)`, `W_hat`), and a camp short of food or water (`F W < 1`) also splits when its leaving group, the `n_short = ceil(N (1 - F W))` people its land cannot carry (clamped to the legal daughter range), would gain at least `G*` at the viable destination (`G_d = Q_d / Q_now - 1` at the parent's expectation, destination support `F_d` from the site score); `FissionReport.Trigger` (Crowding / Distress) and `DaughterGain` record which. A fed camp is unchanged. Details, calibration and measurements: [Fission.md](Fission.md). Checks: `FissionChecks.cs`.
- **Immigration (WP10b, forward-looking since WP10b-2):** `Immigration.Resolve` runs in the Close phase immediately before `Fission.Resolve`, from the same frozen post-demography snapshot, and only when `ImmigrationEnabled` (false by default and in OpeningConfig; saves without the field load it false; independent of `FissionEnabled`). A source with at least `ImmigrationSourceFloor + ImmigrationMinimumGroup` residents scores every known camp within `ImmigrationRadius` hexes (the Society's own camps, camps in current vision, and, after the first breakthrough, present unmoved contacts whose recorded tile is the camp's tile; Paleolithic Societies use vision only) by the forward utility `dU(M) = w_Q (Qhat_j(N_j + M) - Qhat_i(N_i - M)) - K_distance d - K_attachment - K_lifestyle - ReturnPenalty` evaluated at the largest group `M_max = min(floor(N ImmigrationMaxFraction), N - ImmigrationSourceFloor)` (`Qhat` is `Fission.QoLModel.ForwardAt`: the committed terms with clothing coverage, housing per head, cohesion, food `F_hat = min(1, (F N + carried) / N')` and water `W_hat = min(1, W N / N')` (only when `W < 1`) at the new headcount `N'`; `carried` is the migrants' per-head stored food over the per-head turn requirement `Consumption * TurnDays / BaseDays` times `M`, zero for the source; lifestyle barrier 0.05 nomadic camp to village, 0.15 village to nomadic camp). It emits at most one intent, for the best camp (ties: higher `dU`, nearer, lower camp ID), sized `floor(N * ImmigrationMaxFraction * clamp((dU(M_max) - theta) / theta, 0, 1))` and at least `ImmigrationMinimumGroup`, and only if `dU` re-evaluated at that size still exceeds `ImmigrationDeadband`. Hysteresis: each camp keeps sparse `RecentMigration` records (other camp, turn, direction), pruned at commit beyond `ImmigrationReturnCooldownTurns`; a flow back to a camp that sent people to the source `age` turns ago (1 to cooldown) pays `ImmigrationReturnPenalty * (cooldown - age + 1) / cooldown`. Intents reconcile once in destination-then-source camp-ID order: a destination accepts the largest group whose forward utility, at its inflow so far and the food already arriving, stays above `ImmigrationDeadband`; the excess stays home and is never rerouted. The commit moves all departures then all arrivals from frozen values: residents (workforce lost in proportion, arrivals gather) and a per-capita share of typed Plant/Animal/Aquatic food, nothing else; both Societies record a `ContactSource.Migration` contact and both camps a `RecentMigration` record. Sources with an intent and destinations with arrivals emit no fission intent that turn. Journal: `TurnRecord.Migrations` (with the penalty paid and the carried food), `DemographicReport.Emigrated`/`Immigrated`. Observations carry nothing new. Rules, scaling, rejected architectures and measurements: [Immigration.md](Immigration.md). Checks: `ImmigrationChecks.cs`.
- **Trade (phase 1: WP7-WP9, AI-to-AI barter of goods and food):** only when `TradeEnabled` (false by default, in OpeningConfig and in saves without it). Goods are data: `Trade.Registry` holds one descriptor per `TradeGood` (Cloth, Animal Fabric, Housing Material, Food), giving its stock parts, its value-curve key and its activity; food is active unless `TradeFoodEnabled` is off. Curves, in the planner's units: clothing `V e^{-k x/N}` shared by Cloth and Animal Fabric; Housing Material on the fixed-Housing curve (zero without Construction and agriculture); food bands on `P = stock + last turn's FoodProduced`: consumption at `v_food`, then feedstock and reserve at `v_reserve` (protected, never given), then growth at `v_growth` (marginal welfare per birth; kept under `TradeFoodSafeOnly`), then surplus at 0. `Trade.Plan` is a pure same-turn handshake computed in `TurnPlanner.Prepare` (identically in `Draft`) from the committed boundary. Eligible AI camps (player != 0, residents, a goods economy, a remembering Society) send ranked want lists (recoverable utility up to target coverage) and give lists (lowest marginal loss), each of length `TradeListLength`, to at most `TradeRequisitionsPerCamp` remembered, unmoved foreign partners within `TradeRange` hexes. Each responder (camp-ID order, then its best gain) evaluates only the W x W listed pairs. It honours at 1:1, counters once at its floor `Gain (1 - t) = (1 + m) Loss` inside the requester's limit, halves the quantity up to three times, or ignores. Here `t = TradeDistanceCostPerHex · distance` and kin use `m (1 - affinity)`. Cost is `O(camps (G log W + contacts) + camps K W^2)`. The planner and prepared needs (`OpeningCampFood`, bucket targets, feedstock gaps) see post-trade goods and typed food. Goods with a recipe (Cloth, Animal Fabric, Housing Material) may be sold made to order. The cost is `LabourPerUnit · w_lagged + food inputs · v_food` (`EconomicLaborPlan.LabourPrice`, recorded only while trade is on); capacity is `TradeExportLabourShare` of residents' turn, bounded by spare typed food inputs; giving and receiving are both valued on the make-or-buy curve `W'` (use value shifted by remaining making capacity below the use-equals-cost point, the cost within capacity of it, use value above); a pair never moves one curve both ways in a turn. A seller's agreed deliveries are export claims on its planning view (`Camp.TradeExports`): the Value planner staffs their made-to-order part (`Camp.TradeMake`) first at any fulfilment position (reserving role labour, materials and food inputs), `Demand`/`Forecast` net them, `TurnNeeds.Exports` carries them and the resolver holds owed goods back from recipe inputs. Payment is on delivery: `Trade.Settle` moves food legs into escrow at the opening of resolution, before dispatch; `Trade.SettleAfterProduction` moves goods legs between all camps' production and all camps' needs, then pays each escrowed leg the delivered share and refunds the rest (`DemographicReport.TradeFood`); `Trade.Finish` records them. Food bought with goods therefore reaches the receiver only after this turn's growth plan. Goods legs moved together scale together, every part is conserved, `ContactSource.Trade` is recorded both ways and the inert `RecordTradePractice` is credited. Journal: `TurnRecord.Trades`/`TradeActivity`; nothing pending is saved, and there are no order kinds or observation fields yet. [Trade.md](Trade.md); checks: `TradeChecks.cs`.
- **Secondary technologies and the practice API (Husbandry settling, part A):** `TechnologyDiscovery.AddPractice(society, domain, amount, PracticeSource)` is the only writer of domain practice (sources `Gathering`, `Hunting`, `Fishing`, `Trade`; the call site in `GenerationalEconomy.AwardLifestyleProgress` keeps its amounts). It always credits `Society.Experience` (first breakthrough, practice bonuses) and, once the Society's era has left the Paleolithic, also the saved optional `Society.NeolithicExperience` (`double[3]` by `Domain`; absent in older saves, which load zeros; fission daughters copy it). `TechnologyDiscovery.EffectiveProgress`, `Statuses` and `Resolve` grant a secondary technology when Neolithic practice reaches `SecondaryTechnologyThreshold` (default 225 = `ExperienceThreshold`; an unacquired technology's status shows its Neolithic progress and that threshold). The forced-transition Society's `SecondaryDomain` keeps today's carryover `max(CarryoverFloor, CarryoverMultiplier x lifetime experience)`. `RecordTradePractice(config, receiver, domain, goodsValue)` credits `goodsValue x TradePracticePerValue` (0 = inert; no caller yet) through `AddPractice`. Checks: `SettlingChecks.cs`, `FoundationChecks.cs`.
- **Herds against fields (Husbandry settling, part B; Value rule only):** the demand pass (`CampProductionDemand.Build`) forecasts the herd's next-turn growth (`TurnResolver.ForecastGrownManagedAnimalFood`: fully nourished herds grow by `exp(GrowthRate x ManagedBreedingIntensity x years)`, then the grown herd is run through the same forage/water forecast with the herders it needs) and credits the net added food (animal food less human forage displaced) against the food gap before the expansion ladder and the planner's expansion gate are evaluated (`HerdGrowthFood`). A non-village camp whose first technology is Animal Husbandry is charged a settling cost on the ladder step at which its footprint-average field intensity reaches the village threshold (0.25): `SettledHerdGrowthPenalty` (0.35) x the food value of its forecast grown-herd food over the capital life; if the crossing tail is worth less than the cost the ladder stops before the crossing step and the planner caps expansion farmers at the worker-years of the steps before it (`ExpansionEffortCap`). Herd growth is not labour-limited (breeding uses intensity and nutrition only), so no separate herd-expansion claim exists. Nothing is saved; Uniform is unchanged. Checks: `SettlingChecks.cs`. Rules, measurements and limits: [HusbandrySettling.md](HusbandrySettling.md).
- **QoL-dependent mortality (`Docs/QoLMortality.md`):** births stay fixed at `BaseBirthRate` times a fertility hook; deaths are `d0 * MortalityFactor * Q^-a` with `d0 = BaseBirthRate - AnnualHumanGrowthRate`, so the annual growth ceiling is `r = max(0, b0 F - d(Q))`, equals `AnnualHumanGrowthRate` at `Q = 1` and is zero at and below `Q* = (d0/b0)^(1/a)`. Food still limits growth below `r`; `DemographicGrowthMultiplier` and the `DeficitTurns > 0` rule still apply. `QoLMortality.MaximumRate` is the one ceiling shared by `PlanGrowth`, `FoodBucketTargets`, `PlanningResidentPopulation` and the Value rule's `FoodPerBirth`. It uses the camp's committed `Camp.QoL` and applies only to camps with Cultivation or Husbandry (a Paleolithic camp's QoL is capped near 0.75 by construction, so its growth is unchanged). Implied deaths are not recorded; the demographic explanation names the reduction. Lever `QoLMortalityEnabled` is on for new worlds; saves without it load off. A fission daughter now starts at its founding QoL estimate instead of the unset 1.0. Checks: `QoLMortalityChecks.cs`. **Opt-in** (`QoLMortalityEnabled` false by default) until the elasticity is chosen: at a = 2 it meets the size targets but cuts population by up to 92%.
- **Herd-labour coupling (`Docs/HerdLabour.md`):** a camp owns a chosen managed-herd size, `Camp.HerdTarget` (set by the Value planner each automated plan, or by the player through the Economy order; unset means no slaughter). The planner no longer reserves every herder the herd needs first: under the Value rule each herder is a step of a ladder valued in the `ExpansionValuation` food buckets (plus the gatherer- or farmer-years its food saves) and staffed while its break-even price is at least the labour price `w`, with `MinimumHerdHerders` always kept; `HerdTarget = floor(min(grown herd, herders staffed x animals per herder))`, and breeding intensity is capped so the herd is not bred past it. After breeding, `HerdLabour.Slaughter` removes the animals above the target (integer, herds in id order, proportional) and credits `SpeciesDefinition.FoodYield` carcass food per animal to the camp's Animal Food as a realized flow with no ecology pressure (`ManagedHerdReport` and `DemographicReport` `Slaughtered`/`SlaughterFood`). Lever `HerdLabourEnabled` is opt-in (false by default and in saves without it) pending the candidate review and HV3 acceptance in `Docs/HerdValuationValidation.md`. Measured: no camp ends with every resident herding (44 of 106, and 156 of 1,144 with fission, before), at the price of small herds once a camp holds fields. Checks: `HerdLabourChecks.cs`.
- **Camp spacing:** `MinimumCampSpacing` (3 hexes, `SimulationConfig`, validated >= 1) governs movement. `CampPlacement.Resolve` rolls movers back to a fixed point, with a stayer winning and then the lower ID, until every pair of resolved camp destinations with at least one mover is at least the spacing apart (wrapping `HexMap.Distance`). Pre-existing closer pairs may stay; hunter groups are unaffected. `OrderRules.Validate`, `LegalActions` and the heuristic policy refuse destinations within spacing of camps in the actor's own observation (own camps and visible foreign camps); unseen camps are enforced only at resolution. Saves without the field load the default 3. Fission destinations (WP10a) reuse this rule. Checks: `CampSpacingChecks.cs`.
- **Vegetation:** raw `CurrentVegetation` and effective cultivation cover are separate. Cultivation retention must not be presented as rewriting the raw ecological tier. No forest-maintenance discount is authorized.
- **Resource entitlement:** canonical tile order is an implementation constraint, not an approved claim-priority rule. #34 remains open.
- **Social economy (cohesion multiplier implemented, derived from housing; fission, trade etc. remain design):** QoL is `Q = F · W · R · S(N)` in `NeolithicProduction.ResolveNeeds`, with `S(N) = exp(-(N / N_s)^β)` and the cohesion scale derived from the camp's housing, `N_s = N_band (1 + k H)`, `H = min(1, (fixed people + p_tent · tent people) / N)` (`NeolithicProduction.HousingCapacity`/`HousingShare`/`SocialCohesion`; no technology or regime label; tents shelter `LightHousingPeoplePerUnit` people, fixed Housing `HousingPeoplePerUnit`; [SocialCohesion.md](SocialCohesion.md) holds the rule, onset tables and calibration sweep); growth stays independent of QoL. Loaded older saves ignore the retired `CohesionScalePaleolithic/Nomadic/Agricultural` fields and continue under the derived rule. The rest is design: [QoLFissionTradeDesign.md](QoLFissionTradeDesign.md) sets the future QoL formula `Q = B · R · S(N)`, fission, immigration, food trade and proto-towns; [ValueModelDesign.md](ValueModelDesign.md) sets value-based fulfilment and trade; [ValueModelImplementationPlan.md](ValueModelImplementationPlan.md) orders the work. Value-based fulfilment (WP3) exists as `ValueFulfilment` in `ProductionDemand.cs`, selected by the string config `PlannerFulfilment` (`"Uniform"` default, `"Value"`); it prices Cloth, Animal Fabric, Housing and Light Housing only, orders the planner's material-access loops by marginal value, and leaves the resolver's production order unchanged. Fission, immigration and trade (phase 1, goods and food barter) are implemented opt-in (see above); `B` in the design formula is the existing `F · W` product.
- **Planner fulfilment rule:** `NeolithicConfig.PlannerFulfilment` defaults to `"Value"` (code initializer and `OpeningConfig.json`): the labour-price fulfilment rule and value-driven production order in `EconomicPlanner.cs`/`ProductionDemand.cs` (`Docs/EconomicAutomation.md`, `Docs/ValueModelDesign.md`). Saves written before the field existed load as `"Uniform"` (the `OnDeserializing` hook) and continue exactly; `"Uniform"` remains selectable and is pinned by the planner-contract fixtures. Covered by `ValueFulfilmentChecks` and `ProductionOrderChecks`.
- **Unresolved queue:** #21 is closed; #26–#34 are open at this baseline. Existing open work also includes #23/#24/#9–#13/#15. Each implementation must follow its issue contract and the approved architecture above; code presence alone does not settle a disputed rule.
