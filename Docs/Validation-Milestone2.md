# Validation — milestone two, September 9, 2026

Environment: Windows x64, Unity 6000.5.10f1, Unity-bundled .NET SDK 8.0.318. Measurements are local development observations, not target-hardware guarantees.

## Automated results

- 29 original focused assertions plus 57 milestone-two assertions passed. New checks exercise impassable supply barriers, movement beyond supply range, provision withdrawal/retention/capacity/deposit, isolated starvation, duration/reserve-gated growth, explicit fresh-only drinking, lake-bank access, marine food renewal, oasis depletion, stable species practice, layered observations and persistence of nonzero expedition/growth/oasis state.
- 30 seeded four-tribe games: 120/120 tribes survived to the era transition, zero illegal AI orders, zero replay mismatches. Replays reversed camp, hunter, herd and submitted-order collection order.
- The suite recorded 47 starvation deaths and 118 people added through net growth. Conservation passed each turn. Survival here means the tribe survived, not that every individual survived.
- First technologies: 1 Animal Husbandry, 8 Cultivation, 111 Watercraft/Fisheries. Hunting remains uncommon as a first technology and this tuning strongly favors fishing; geographic visibility guarantees do not establish balanced strategies.
- Standard map: 524,288 tiles, approximately 7.1 seconds generation and 1.3 seconds for a complete ten-turn AI opening in the headless runtime. Managed memory at the sampled point was 287–289 MB; this is a GC-dependent snapshot, not a peak-memory guarantee.
- Standard save: approximately 1.3 seconds and 33.7 MB compressed. Load: approximately 1.1 seconds. Unity uses the same codec with asynchronous UI integration.
- All 9 Unity EditMode tests passed in the final run. These include actual Resources-loaded tileset/configuration checks, engine compilation, save/replay games and information isolation. The Windows build reported `Build Finished, Result: Success`.

## Before/after audit

Eight Standard Continents worlds, seeds 1–8, six camps each. Baseline was measured from the first playable implementation before the second-milestone changes. The final audit validates coastal classification, river graphs and oasis eligibility/spacing for every generated world.

| Metric | Before | Milestone two |
|---|---:|---:|
| Wild herds per Standard world | 14–46 | 928–1,656 |
| Player opening with a visible herd | 0/8 | 8/8 |
| Nearest herd to player camp | 9–129 hexes | 1–2 hexes |
| All camps with reachable opening herd | No guarantee | 48/48 |
| Ocean tiles illegally adjacent to land | 2,881–5,082 per world | 0 |
| Shared boundary-river edges | None; center-tile marker | 404–2,726 |
| Oasis features | None | 55–103 per world |
| Camel herds | None | 285–698 per world |
| Independent relief | Mountain replaced biome | All four relief bands present |
| Human growth in the 30-seed opening suite | 0 | 118 people |
| Starvation deaths in that suite | 104 | 47 |

After-generation relief counts, in Lowland/Hills/Highland/Mountain order, were 14,840/145,444/22,239/1,559 for seed 1 and 38,271/198,921/30,264/2,634 for seed 2. Every sampled world retained independent biome and elevation data. The shift in deaths cannot be attributed to one feature alone: the generation, wildlife, water, logistics and demographic models changed together.

Across the eight worlds, 372 of 4,083 camel herds (9.1%) spawned in oasis-influenced habitat. Oasis-influenced desert occupied 4,522 of 722,170 desert tiles (0.63%). Each world separately passed the oasis preference regression. Camel desert preference and horse grassland preference also pass direct suitability checks. Global horse distribution includes tundra and woodland because the sampled maps have limited grassland; habitat preference is not an exclusive spawning restriction.

Raw reproducible logs are in ignored `TestResults/milestone2-before.txt`, `milestone2-after.txt`, `milestone2-headless.txt`, `milestone2-unity.xml` and `milestone2-build.log`. The before results are preserved rather than regenerated by newer code.

## Interactive checks

The final Windows development player was exercised through the actual game window. Standard seed 1729 generated with a visible camel herd, separate Desert/Hills labels and explicit freshwater sources. V switched classic/high-contrast glyphs without advancing the season or changing population. Asynchronous version-two save completed; advancing to season two and loading restored season one, population, resources and visibility. The old `opening.anthro` remained alongside `opening-v2.anthro`.

Small seed 43, six tribes, showed a continuous river along shared hex edges. Inspecting a horse herd's bank displayed Tundra/Lowland, River freshwater and a boundary-flow band. Small seed 42 showed a Desert/Hills oasis with its own `o` glyph, freshwater source and finite-spring description; zoom confirmed that biome, relief, feature and food markers remain separately readable. Help displayed the current glyph legend and controls without clipping.

In that oasis opening, eight hunters dispatched, moved two hexes, then moved to four hexes from camp. The sidebar changed from supplied at path range 3 to beyond-supply with carried provisions. Returning to two hexes deposited food and produced an explicit delivery journal entry. The resulting camp deficit and group hunger were visible. The player was then returned to the saved fresh Standard opening. Player logs contained no managed exceptions during these checks.
