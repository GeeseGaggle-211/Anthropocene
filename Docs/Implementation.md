supersedes historical cultivation, manufacturing and QoL descriptions below.

[EcologyFoundation.md](EcologyFoundation.md) is the current mechanics contract; [ConfigurationUnits.md](ConfigurationUnits.md) defines parameter meanings. ## Ownership and boundaries

`Anthropocene.Simulation` is Unity-independent C# 9, compiled unchanged by Unity and the .NET 8 headless runner. `WorldState` owns generated geography, latent ecology, entities, societies, configuration and deterministic history. `Society` owns player identity, mandates, policy preferences, tradition/practice, technology and `EraProgression`. `Camp` is a local population center with people, food, demographic progress and `PopulationLedger`. Hunters identify their source settlement and society; managed herds and improvements have separate society/settlement ownership. One society can share tradition across multiple camps in data/tests; playable daughter-settlement creation is deferred.

The active turn path is `AiPlanning.Decide` -> `TurnPlanner.Prepare` -> `TurnResolver.Resolve(PreparedTurn)`. Preparation validates and copies orders, gives explicit camp orders precedence over automation, and fixes needs for the existing cohort and a `TurnHorizon`. Resolution applies those intents, moves entities, evaluates fixed claims and field efficacy over one horizon, pays realized needs and storage, then commits births. Ordinary observations do not run the economic planner. The compatibility `Resolve(world, orders, traces)` overload prepares before entering the mutating resolver. Prepared turns and the local deposit/source index are transient; load reconstructs them without a save-format change. Each active tile receives one horizon integration with the four seasonal profiles reduced to environmental coefficients. The normative order is in [TurnResolutionPasses.md](TurnResolutionPasses.md), with equations and validation in [TurnHorizonRefactor.md](TurnHorizonRefactor.md).

Food and goods are paid for the prior cohort before demographic losses or births are committed. A turn-level transfer has no subturn timestamp in `TurnRecord`; canonical history combines matching tile/entity/kind receipts after all turn effects and demographic reports are calculated. Large local 320-tribe saves may exceed the former 128 MiB binary XML metadata guard; the loader keeps a finite 512 MiB bound without changing the envelope or resource blocks.

`Ownership.Bind` is an explicit construction/load/resolution boundary. It installs shared society references; observation, projection and saving must not create owners or IDs. Compatibility accessors on Camp delegate to Society and are not separately serialized histories. `PracticeEffects` derives task efficiency from society-owned practice; future specialist proficiency can replace that policy without moving ownership again.

`OpeningTransitions` records owned transition seeds and optional immediate opening rewards. Only the fishery flag retains the compatibility food multiplier; Cultivated grants no flat food bonus. Five starting domestic animals retain their existing opening behavior. Physical cultivation now uses E/q and the shared ecology/labor model in `CultivationEconomy`. Era completion belongs to society state; the opening ends when each society has completed it or has no surviving people.

After the first breakthrough, `TechnologyDiscovery` evaluates the same society-owned Hunting, Gathering and Fishing practice at the turn's Close boundary. Each unowned domain reaches its corresponding Husbandry, Cultivation or Watercraft capability at the configured 225-point threshold; the existing forced-transition carryover applies only to the selected secondary domain. The first breakthrough's majority and forced deadlines never apply to later acquisitions. Later knowledge changes only technology flags. It does not call `OpeningTransitions` or create animals, fields, fisheries or other opening compatibility stock. The initial Watercraft first transition retains its existing one-time, eligible `FisherySeed` compatibility reward; secondary acquisitions do not create or repeat it. Newly acquired capabilities enter the next prepared turn. Own-only progress, threshold, acquired state and blockers are included in observation schema 15 and the player sidebar. The opening reward's persisted processed marker migrates completed older worlds, including those without a transition seed, without replacing deliberate false on fresh worlds.

## Generation

The dependency order is Geometry -> Warped tectonic provinces -> Continuous continental fields, relief and hotspot tracks -> Two-dimensional atmospheric transport -> Basin retention and spillway incision -> Boundary rivers -> Elevation levels -> Soil & Environment -> Oases -> Vegetation & Seasonal Resources -> Placement. [WorldGeneration.md](WorldGeneration.md) records the generator-9 replacement; [ArchipelagoRefinement.md](ArchipelagoRefinement.md) records generator 10's fragmented crust and moving historical uplift windows. Coastlines are independent of categorical plate ownership; climate travel is normalized to planetary distance; hydrological routing corrections are not automatically lakes. Hotspot trajectory samples are transient generation inputs; only the resulting elevation/activity is authoritative and persisted.

Climate creates four fixed seasonal profiles. EcologyStage independently accumulates regional climatic moisture and hydrologically subsidized local soil moisture. Only the former classifies land environment. Oases alter local soil/capacities before seasonal resources finish; natural vegetation then uses climatic potential plus a freshwater-weighted local uplift capped at Medium. High requires regional support. This is a small ordering change inside VegetationResourceStage, before gatherable-share initialization; the stage sequence is unchanged. Environment, current/natural vegetation and relief/elevation remain independent dimensions. Land food capacity and recovery use local soil water; vegetation changes accessible share without multiplying total productivity a second time. Elevation level 10 excludes camps, hunters and herds; marine level 0, lake freshness, coastlines and shared river topology retain their invariants. Exact generation equations and provisional constants are in [EcologyFoundation.md](EcologyFoundation.md).

Nearby opening herds, regional density bonuses, viable-start filtering, coarse cold-steppe habitat and disabled-by-default wild-herd fission remain explicit opening balance scaffolds. The generator is not a historical reconstruction or a complete climate/ecosystem model.

`AquaticEcology` generates marine/lake seasonal forcing and the generator-11 [aquatic food web](AquaticFoodWeb.md): separate food/biomass shares, shallow light advantage, deep-ocean biomass and ocean-contact upwelling restricted to outer-shelf Shallows with no adjacent land. Persisted seasonal rates determine aquatic condition recovery without terrestrial vegetation penalties; aquatic vegetation means plankton/algae and responds to aquatic condition. Ocean remains outside opening harvest reach. This reuses existing state and save fields; climate and upwelling forcing are not recomputed during play or loading.

`PlacementStage.Viability` evaluates four-season recurring human food, using the same starting workforce share and a single land/lake/coastal labor ceiling per season. It requires nonsaline capacity in every season and mean renewable freshwater; Ocean, peaks, initial stock windfalls and inaccessible forage biomass cannot inflate eligibility. The score is percent of starting food need (default threshold 85). This consumes completed local land and aquatic profiles without materialization; it preserves geographic differences and separate spacing/prey checks. See EcologyFoundation.md for the equations and limits of this estimate.

## One simultaneous generation

[TurnResolutionPasses.md](TurnResolutionPasses.md) is the authoritative pass
contract. It names the preparation, movement, resource, needs and close
boundaries; specifies permitted writes; and locks goods and QoL to the prior
population cohort. Preserve that one-way order when changing economy or
ecology code. Hunting carcass food remains distinct from plant biomass
extraction; only realized flows cause ecological pressure. Four seasonal
climate profiles parameterize one resource transition and create no
authoritative intermediate state.

## Sparse ecology and parallel execution

`EcologyMath` implements bounded exponential stock/condition updates, analytic depletion and recharge, and affine composition of seasonal forcing for quiet tiles. `EcologicalPressure` resolves the coupled human-accessible and non-gatherable pools plus finite freshwater once per active tile and turn horizon. Claimants debit one nonnegative tile supply in stable entity order; an earlier claimant can exhaust it. Each affected tile returns an endpoint state, transfers, pressure entries and a mass-balance record.

Quiet tiles store `LastEcologyUpdateDays` and latent stocks/condition/tier. `LazyEcology.ProjectEcology` is pure. Quiet full cycles compose algebraically, with bounded partial-cycle work and vegetation hysteresis at the endpoint. `MaterializeEcology` commits that projection before an active change. Neither visibility nor diagnostic query order can alter a fingerprint. Transient ledgers exist only for active tiles and the latest resolved turn, not as per-tile heap objects or permanent save history.

`PlanningIndex` maps occupants by tile/player/source and indexes recent extraction once. Bounded local path/supply queries and cached temporary ecology views serve the planning phase. `PhaseExecution` provides a scalar reference and optional workers for independent AI intentions, herd intentions and active-tile calculations. Workers return stable output slots; canonical barriers own entity changes, IDs, collisions and history. Worker counts, partition order and timing are runtime diagnostics outside saved state. Desktop execution uses at most four workers with a small-work threshold.

The 320-tribe configuration is benchmark-only. Dense Tile/SeasonData storage still needs profiling/compaction before that size becomes a public preset; see the measured memory and scaling limits in [Performance.md](Performance.md).

## Human/AI information and actions

`AgentObservationBuilder` constructs immutable, currently visible information for the player and each policy. It exposes exact owned populations/livestock, estimated wild/foreign herd counts and qualitative resource bands. Hidden tiles, foreign stores/populations, global logs and submitted rival orders remain unavailable. Productivity/accessibility and vegetation use projected current state.

`OrderRules` is the shared legality contract. `LegalActions` supplies masks, bounded dispatch ranges and enumerated legal candidates. `ObservationDto` and action DTOs are serializable primitive contracts at schema 11; owned domestic-herd diagnostics, breeding policy, recent own movement and pastoral inventories are included, while foreign and wild private state remains filtered. Serialization does not grant extra information. The heuristic policy consumes those filtered observations. Persistent inclinations are seeded from world/origin state, not player-number cohorts; mandates, visible opportunity, actual food history and relocation inertia determine choices. There is no culture selection or RL training.

## Presentation and persistence

### Neolithic realignment and saved automation — September 20, 2026

For current Neolithic mechanics, [NeolithicRealignment.md](NeolithicRealignment.md) and [EconomicAutomation.md](EconomicAutomation.md) supersede historical opening-only/resource-deferred statements below. `EconomicPlanner` proposes source-, tool-, food- and demand-constrained staffing for farmers, supplementary gathering, matching specialists and separate expansion. Player and AI use validated economic intents. `EconomyAutomationTurns` persists Off/next-N/continuous operation; optional `EconomicLaborPlan` metadata protects material gathering, expansion and useful next-cycle equipment demand. Clay Miner/Pottery and the Stone Quarrier display label are supported. Fabric feedstock cannot also fund demographic growth. Current saves use the compatible `ANTHROPOCENE-11` envelope with real material-source ledgers and authoritative E/operator state; this automation addition changes metadata only, not tile/resource layouts. See [NeolithicAutomationResults.md](NeolithicAutomationResults.md) for measured behavior and remaining forecast limitations.

`OpeningGame` owns the Unity immediate-mode menu, planning controls and worker busy guards. The renderer draws only the camera region: ground -> elevation contours -> water edges -> vegetation -> land use -> occupants -> resource/water badges and UI feedback. It uses 29 original cartographic sprites with semantic glyph fallbacks, two contrast sets and zoom-dependent detail. Sprite colour is separate from fallback glyph tint. Rendering and hit testing share stable occupant slots; selections use open corner marks and have no opaque backing panels. Shorelines use observed neighbour land only. Delayed hover uses filtered information, resets with navigation and never takes input.

Generation, resolution/AI planning and save/load run on workers. The UI shows an operation/progress screen and does not inspect partially resolved state. `SaveCodec` stores authoritative binary geography and versioned metadata in buffered gzip; loading never regenerates geography. Current Neolithic saves use `ANTHROPOCENE-11`; the separate `ResourceCodec` block was introduced with the `ANTHROPOCENE-9` envelope, and older `ANTHROPOCENE-8` worlds remain readable with no resource layer. World/config version 7 and core generator version 11 remain unchanged; the observation contract advanced from schema 8 to schema 9 for owned domestic-herd diagnostics, schema 10 for managed-breeding policy and recent own movement, and schema 11 for owned pastoral inventories, while the filename remains `opening-v7.anthro`. Fixed agricultural Housing is saved once by owning camp and tile with its elapsed-time clock; older carried Housing migrates to that camp's saved tile. Generator-7 through generator-10 saves retain stored ecology and original aquatic recovery. Earlier formats without separate regional moisture remain rejected. Pending orders and transient diagnostics are excluded. [GeologicalResources.md](GeologicalResources.md) describes the independent resource generator/schema, saved access metadata and diagnostic maps; the opening economy and player observations do not use the new layer.

Unity EditMode tests cover engine integration and presentation contracts. The optional development-player `--anthropocene-smoke-output <directory>` route exercises the real UI helpers and captures rendered frames, saving only to its own output directory. The dedicated `--anthropocene-husbandry-smoke-output <directory>` route uses a synthetic Husbandry-only camp to verify the shared Economy Herder order, owned managed-herd diagnostics, breeding intensity, save/load and two-turn automation. Neither route overwrites the player's normal save slot. The Screen Capture module is included for these validation routes; original artwork and importer provenance are in [ArtProvenance.md](ArtProvenance.md).

Managed breeding is a camp policy in the shared `EconomicIntent`: new camps start at .55, and players may set `b` from 0 to 1. The managed-herd exponent is `GrowthRate * b * current nutrition * elapsed years`. Loading defaults to .55 only when a save predates this field; a present invalid value is rejected and a saved zero remains a deliberate zero. Ordinary economy automation carries the camp's chosen value forward. The heuristic submits the same validated intent using only its owned herd diagnostics and visible terrain: .55 in the healthy band; .45 below .95 nutrition, .80 management, adequate current pasture (band 2), or three traversable adjacent adequate tiles, or above a .60 Herder share; and .65 only after at least 25% owned-herd loss or a real recent move, with .80 management, at most .60 Herder share, ample occupied pasture (band 3) and three adjacent adequate tiles. These are provisional policy heuristics, not biological reproduction constants.

`Tools/Headless/HusbandryChecks.cs` is the focused managed-herd contract suite and is
invoked by both `--neolithic-only` and `--economy-only`. It covers source-ledger
conservation, shared human/herd starvation, species-order replay, newborn chronology,
common useful-food provenance and storage acceptance; it does not replace Unity
presentation validation.
